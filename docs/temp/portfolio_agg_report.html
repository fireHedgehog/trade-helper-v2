What this is

The frozen per-symbol signal (entry cluster, c3_d20 exit, long-only except Bond ETFs + BTC) is turned into one equity curve by a rule ladder. Each variant adds one risk layer; the goal is the simplest variant that captures the tail benefit, not the highest CAGR. Portfolio return = Σ wi(t-1)·strat_reti(t) − rebalance cost; weights use only past data.

  • P0 equal-notional · P1 inverse-vol · P2 +vol-target scalar · P3 +position/gross caps · P4 +fixed sleeve risk budgets · P4c +correlation haircut (robustness)
  • Reported at k_max = 1.0 (unlevered) and k_max = 2.0 (moderate). Two universes: full, and restricted (no single-name equities) as the survivorship control.
